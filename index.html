About RCA

A national academy built for the technology generation

Rwanda Coding Academy is a specialized secondary institution dedicated to training young Rwandans in software engineering and computer science, giving students a rigorous, hands-on foundation in how modern technology is built.

Technology education matters because it turns students from consumers of digital tools into creators of them — able to build software, secure systems, and design the infrastructure that Rwanda's economy will run on.

Through structured study and continuous practice, students at RCA develop programming, problem-solving, systems thinking and collaboration skills that carry directly into university study and the technology workforce.

LearnFoundational and advanced computing knowledge, taught from first principles.
BuildReal software projects that turn theory into working products.
CollaborateTeamwork, code review and shared problem-solving as daily practice.
InnovateApplying new tools — AI included — to Rwanda's own challenges.
